The Global Mineral Challenge

Why Manganese Intelligence Matters

Manganese is critical for steel manufacturing. Traditional mineral exploration can be expensive, slow and difficult to scale.

Increasing Demand

Indispensable for global steel production and surging rapidly as a key cathode material in next-generation electric vehicle batteries and energy storage systems.

Limited Exploration

Conventional ground geological surveys and exploratory core drillings are labor-intensive, geographically restricted, highly costly, and difficult to scale globally.

Production Uncertainty

Declining ore grades in mature open-pit and underground mines, combined with unpredictable geotechnical and structural variations, create high output volatility.

Supply Shortfall Risk

Heavy geopolitical concentration of high-grade reserves creates severe supply chain vulnerabilities and projected multi-million-tonne annual deficits by 2027.

Hyperspectral Geological Prospecting

AI-Driven Manganese Reserve Discovery

MnVision AI analyzes multi-satellite constellations (Sentinel-2, Cartosat, RISAT SAR, Landsat-9) to detect pyrolusite, psilomelane, and braunite mineralization signatures concealed beneath overburden and vegetation.

SWIR Band Ratio Indexing

Calculates precise hydroxyl absorption at 2.20 µm and 2.35 µm to differentiate high-grade manganese oxide mineralization from iron-laterite host rock.

SAR Structural Penetration

X-band synthetic aperture radar maps subsurface faults, folds, and gondite stratigraphic contacts to uncover blind deposits with zero surface outcrop.

Automated Anomaly Scoring

Ensemble convolutional neural networks rank exploration targets across India, Africa, and Australia with confidence metrics and 3D geological depth estimates.
